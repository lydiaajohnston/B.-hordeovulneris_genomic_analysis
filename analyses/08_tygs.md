Purpose 
The feline and canine assemblies were analysed using the TYGS (Type Strain Genome Server) 
to investigate their taxonomic placement and identify their closest type strains.

Method 

TYGS was assessed via webserver rather than command line 
The feline SPAdes assembly (spades_cat/contigs.fasta) was submitted to TYGS for taxonomic analysis.

TYGS generated:

Genome-to-genome distance comparisons
dDDH estimates
16S rRNA gene phylogenetic analysis
Whole-genome phylogenetic analysis

Results 
The closest type strain identified by TYGS was Buchananella hordeovulneris DSM 20732.

For the feline isolate, the reported dDDH values against DSM 20732 were:

Measure	Result
dDDH d0	86.7%
dDDH d4	32.1%
dDDH d6	71.7%
G+C difference	1.76%

TYGS reported:
Potential new species detected: your strain 'contigs' does not belong to any species found in TYGS data base

The feline isolate showed phylogenetic affinity with B. hordeovulneris in both the 16S rRNA and whole-genome trees.

Interpretation 
The TYGS results indicate that the feline isolate is most closely related to B. hordeovulneris DSM 20732 among 
the type strains available in the TYGS database. However, the results did not allow the feline isolate to be 
confidently assigned to B. hordeovulneris.

The discrepancy between the dDDH measures and the relatively low genome alignment observed against DSM 20732 
suggested that further genomic comparison was required.

Therefore, additional B. hordeovulneris genomes were sought from NCBI for comparison using FastANI.
